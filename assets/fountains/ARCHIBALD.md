# Archibald visual refinement

Original stylised vector artwork inspired by Apollo with his lyre and outstretched arm, Diana and hound, Theseus and Minotaur, and smaller Pan, horse-head and turtle-like bronze reliefs. Red granite, fluting, stepped trim and water highlights distinguish the finale from earlier fountains. The side groups occupy their existing collision masses; smaller forms are inset reliefs, not new obstacles.
